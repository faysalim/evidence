## Warm-up (2 min)

- What is your role, and how does `<the task area>` fit into it?

## The last time (15 min) — the core of the session

- Walk me through the **last time** you had to `<do the thing>`. Start from what prompted it.
- What did you do first? Then what?
- Where did it go wrong, or take longer than you expected?
- What did you have open at the same time?

## Cost and workarounds (10 min)

- Is there anything you have set up, or anyone you ask, to make this easier?
  *(The workaround is the finding. People will not volunteer it.)*
- How long does this take you now? How often?
- What have you already tried and stopped using? What made you stop?
- What happens if it does not get done?

## Boundaries (5 min)

- Who else is affected when this goes wrong?
- Is there a version of this task you deliberately avoid?

## Close

- If one thing about this changed next month, what would you want it to be?
  *(Ask last. Treat the answer as a wish, not a requirement.)*
- Anything I should have asked?

## Do not ask

- "Would you use a feature that…" — everyone says yes.
- "How do you usually…" — the general case is a reconstruction; the last time is a memory.
- Anything that describes the solution before the end. If you do show it, note the timestamp: everything
  after it is contaminated and the synthesis must say so.
